Design decisions

Decision Rationale Tradeoff Evidence
Share Trader lifecycle across real and simulated sessions. Common order-state machinery keeps host interfaces consistent. Venue-created/self-managed/simulated branches have different confirmation behavior. Trader._create_new_order (L332) / TraderSimulator.init (L36)
Treat pending exchange creation as distinct state. Asynchronous exchanges can acknowledge submission before order becomes live. Waiting requires timeout and synchronization policy; submission alone is not fill proof. Trader._create_new_order (L332) / OrdersManager.register_pending_creation_order (L166)

Workflow

Create a real or simulated order

Detailed fills and portfolio updates are order-state callbacks; sequence does not claim direct synchronous persistence after creation. Concrete strategies/exchange overrides come from tentacles.

Strengths and limits

Strengths

  • Pending creation registration plus explicit wait controls distinguish exchange submission from active-order confirmation. Trader._create_new_order (L332) / OrdersManager.register_pending_creation_order (L166)

Limitations

  • Concrete connector capability/precision/order restrictions and activated trading mode determine usable venue behavior. ExchangeBuilder._build_exchange_manager (L58) / CCXTConnector.create_market_buy_order (L728)
